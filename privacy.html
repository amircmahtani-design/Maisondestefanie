Privacy & cookies

We keep this simple, and we collect nothing about you.

This site has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Nobody is watching what you read here, and nothing about your visit is sent to us.

What is stored on your device

A few small preferences are saved by your own browser and never leave it:

  • which language you chose;
  • whether you asked for the map to load.

Clearing your browser data removes them. Nothing is stored that could identify you.

The map

The map at the bottom of the page comes from Google Maps, which sets its own cookies. That is why it does not load unless you press the button asking for it. If you never press it, Google is never contacted.

Fonts

The typefaces on this site are served by Google Fonts, which records the request in its own server logs. If you would rather that did not happen, most browsers let you block third-party requests.

If you message us

When you write to us on WhatsApp or Instagram, or call the shop, that conversation lives with WhatsApp, Instagram or your phone company under their own terms, not ours. We keep the message only for as long as it takes to answer you and, if you order something, to make and send it.

Bespoke formulas

If Stefanie composes a perfume for you, the formula and your first name are kept on file so it can be made again when you ask. Tell us and it is deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be erased. Message us on Instagram or call the shop on +30 694 747 8033 and it will be dealt with.
