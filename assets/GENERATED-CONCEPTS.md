# Generated concept artwork

The generated images in this folder and the matching responsive WebPs
in assets/img are concept artwork for the current visual direction. They
are not documentary photographs and do not verify actual packaging,
products, shop details, or location details. Keep the warm single-side
light and dark surface when replacing them with real photography.

The twelve shelf objects share one surface, crop and light treatment.
Sources: assets/source/generated-products/product-p1.jpg through
product-p12.jpg (1120 × 1400 px, upright 4:5).
