La Maison de Stefanie — the assets folder
=========================================

Everything in assets/img is TEMPORARY. It is original artwork, rendered
for this build so the layout could be judged with real pictures in it.
None of it is a photograph and none of it is stock. It is there to be
replaced by Stefanie's own photography.

2. THE PICTURES STILL STANDING IN
---------------------------------
Nine of the twelve picture slots are now hers. Three are not, and these
are the ones worth shooting first. Go to Studio -> Pictures, upload the
real one, and it takes over; the temporary file stops being used.

  Wide shot of the shop   the room from the doorway, the whole bench in
                          frame. Runs the full width of the page, so
                          shoot it WIDE — landscape, roughly 21:9 of
                          usable picture. 2400px on the long edge.

  The shopfront           Mnisikleous 10 from the street, at dusk, light
                          coming out of the door. Upright, 4:5.
                          1600px on the long edge.

  Plaka                   the steps just outside, the neighbourhood.
                          Upright, 4:5. 1600px on the long edge.

Already hers and not to be re-shot unless she wants to: the portrait,
the rose, the essence bowl, the copper still, the sealed bottle, and the
nine bottle frames.

Shoot dark. One warm light source off to one side, everything else
falling away into shadow. Do not light the room evenly — the whole site
is built around that contrast and a flat, brightly lit photograph will
look wrong in it.

4. THE HERO FILM (optional)
---------------------------
If there is a loop of her working, drop it in as:

  assets/stefanie_hero_loop.webm         desktop, under about 4 MB
  assets/stefanie_hero_loop_mobile.webm  phone, under about 1.5 MB

then type the filenames into Studio -> Pictures -> The hero film. With no
film the hero still is used and the page is faster. The film never loads
for anyone on a data-saver connection or with reduced motion turned on.


5. THE SOURCE PHOTOGRAPHS
-------------------------
tools/supplied is where her full-resolution originals go, and it is in
.gitignore, which means a rebuilt machine loses them. Commit the
originals of anything that matters. The derived WebP files in assets/img
are committed and safe, but without the originals nothing can be
re-cropped or re-graded.


6. A NOTE ON THE PRELOAD
------------------------
index.html preloads the hero still by name so it starts downloading
immediately. If you change the hero photograph, update that one <link
rel="preload"> line near the top of index.html to match, or simply
delete it — the page works either way, it is only a head start.
