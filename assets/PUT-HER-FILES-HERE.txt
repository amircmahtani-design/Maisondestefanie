La Maison de Stefanie — the assets folder
=========================================

Nothing in this folder is hers yet. Everything here is a placeholder,
generated so the layout can be reviewed. Each placeholder names, on the
image itself, the photograph that belongs in its place.


1. HER ICON — do this first, and do not rename anything
-------------------------------------------------------
The favicon is her logo. Copy these exact filenames out of the current
live site (open lamaisondestefanie.com, View Source, and look at the
<link rel="icon"> tags — or just download them straight from
lamaisondestefanie.com/favicon.ico and so on):

  /favicon.ico                     <- goes in the TOP level, not in here
  assets/favicon.svg
  assets/favicon-32x32.png
  assets/favicon-16x16.png
  assets/apple-touch-icon.png      180x180
  assets/android-chrome-192x192.png
  assets/android-chrome-512x512.png
  assets/og-image.jpg              1200x630, used by Google and WhatsApp

Until these are copied across the browser tab has no icon and links
shared on WhatsApp or Instagram show no picture.


2. THE PHOTOGRAPHS
------------------
Replace each placeholder with a real photograph, keeping the same name
but the real extension (.jpg, or .webp if you have it), then point the
Studio at it:  Studio -> Pictures -> the matching slot.

  placeholder-hero-still.svg       the whole first screen, wide and dark
  placeholder-hero-bottle.svg      one filled bottle, side-lit, on black
  placeholder-hero-botanical.svg   a rose or raw botanical, close and low
  placeholder-hero-vessel.svg      the glass blending bowl with oil in it
  placeholder-portrait.svg         Stefanie at the bench, working
  placeholder-counter.svg          a hand, a pipette, a strip of paper
  placeholder-botanical.svg        petals, resin, mastic
  placeholder-workshop.svg         the organ of bottles behind the counter
  placeholder-sealing.svg          her hands sealing a bottle
  placeholder-atwork.svg           the full room from the doorway
  placeholder-shopfront.svg        Mnisikleous 10 from the street, at dusk
  placeholder-plaka.svg            the steps just outside the door

Shoot dark. One warm light source, off to one side, everything else
falling away into shadow. Do not light the whole room evenly — the site
is built around that contrast and flat photographs sit badly in it.


3. THE HERO FILM (optional)
---------------------------
If there is a loop of her working, drop it in as:

  assets/stefanie_hero_loop.webm         desktop, under about 4 MB
  assets/stefanie_hero_loop_mobile.webm  phone, under about 1.5 MB

then type the filenames into Studio -> Pictures -> The hero film. With no
film the hero still is used instead and the page is faster. The film never
loads on a phone data-saver connection or for anyone who has asked their
device to reduce motion.
