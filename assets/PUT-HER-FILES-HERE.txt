La Maison de Stefanie — the assets folder
=========================================

Everything in assets/img is TEMPORARY. It is original artwork, rendered
for this build so the layout could be judged with real pictures in it.
None of it is a photograph and none of it is stock. It is there to be
replaced by Stefanie's own photography.


1. HER ICON — do this first, and do not rename anything
-------------------------------------------------------
The favicon is her logo. Copy these exact filenames out of the current
live site (open lamaisondestefanie.com, View Source, and look at the
<link rel="icon"> tags — or download them straight from
lamaisondestefanie.com/favicon.ico and so on):

  /favicon.ico                     <- the TOP level, not in here
  assets/favicon.svg
  assets/favicon-32x32.png
  assets/favicon-16x16.png
  assets/apple-touch-icon.png      180x180
  assets/android-chrome-192x192.png
  assets/android-chrome-512x512.png
  assets/og-image.jpg              1200x630, used by Google and WhatsApp

Until these are copied across the browser tab has no icon and links
shared on WhatsApp or Instagram show no picture. These are the only
files on the whole site that cannot be generated.


2. REPLACING THE PICTURES
-------------------------
Go to Studio -> Pictures. Each slot names the photograph it wants and
shows what is standing in for it now. Upload the real one and it takes
over; the temporary file stops being used.

  Hero still          the whole first screen, wide and dark
  Hero — the bottle   one filled bottle, side-lit, on black or cut out
  Hero — botanical    a rose or raw botanical, close and low
  Hero — vessel       the glass blending bowl with oil in it
  Stefanie            her at the bench, working
  Beside her words    a hand, a pipette, a strip of blotting paper
  Raw materials       petals, resin, mastic
  The blending bench  the shelf of essences — shoot WIDE, it runs full width
  Bottled and sealed  a finished bottle being sealed
  Wide shot of shop   the room from the doorway — also full width
  The shopfront       Mnisikleous 10 from the street, at dusk
  Plaka               the steps just outside the door

Shoot dark. One warm light source off to one side, everything else
falling away into shadow. Do not light the room evenly — the whole site
is built around that contrast and a flat, brightly lit photograph will
look wrong in it.

The products on the shelf are the same: Studio -> The shelf -> a product
-> its photograph. Those get the house treatment automatically, so a
phone photo on a plain surface is enough.


3. THE HERO FILM (optional)
---------------------------
If there is a loop of her working, drop it in as:

  assets/stefanie_hero_loop.webm         desktop, under about 4 MB
  assets/stefanie_hero_loop_mobile.webm  phone, under about 1.5 MB

then type the filenames into Studio -> Pictures -> The hero film. With no
film the hero still is used and the page is faster. The film never loads
for anyone on a data-saver connection or with reduced motion turned on.


4. A NOTE ON THE PRELOAD
------------------------
index.html preloads the hero still by name so it starts downloading
immediately. If you change the hero photograph, update that one <link
rel="preload"> line near the top of index.html to match, or simply
delete it — the page works either way, it is only a head start.
