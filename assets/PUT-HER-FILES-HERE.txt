La Maison de Stefanie — asset guide
==================================

The site is built around one visual language: near-black surroundings,
warm ivory, antique gold, and one warm source of light falling across
the subject. The hero story stays rose → essence → copper still →
finished bottle with petals moving through the frame.

THE SITE IS SIX PAGES
---------------------
index.html, maison.html, bespoke.html, story.html, creations.html and
visit.html. They are GENERATED. Do not edit them by hand — the shell is
tools/shell.html, the sections are tools/sections.html, and after
changing either you run:

  python3 tools/pages.py

The stylesheet is assets/site.css and the script is assets/site.js, both
shared by all six.

HOW SHARP ANY OF THIS CAN BE
----------------------------
Every derived image is written by a script, and each script is now sized
from its own master rather than to a round number, because a photograph
enlarged past its own pixels only costs bytes.

  tools/heroes.py      the six page openings, from the 1672 x 941 PNGs.
                       WebP quality 86, up from 74. The PNG is the
                       ceiling; nothing is enlarged.
  tools/widescenes.py  the four home-page chapters and the nine bottle
                       frames. The canvas is now as tall as the master,
                       so the subject is never resampled, at quality 82
                       rather than 72. Measured on the subject panel
                       alone that is 1.3 to 1.4 times the detail.
  tools/filmplates.py  the first-screen film's plates, from the 1280 x 720
                       motion pack. Resize once, unsharp mask, quality 90.
  tools/portrait.py    her photograph, from the 1400 x 939 original.
                       Quality 90.

What none of them can do is invent detail. The motion pack is 720p and
the page artwork is 1672px wide; those are the ceilings. Replace a master
and run its script again — no other file has to change.

PAGE OPENINGS
-------------
Each page opens on one scene composed three times:

  assets/heroes/<page>/desktop.webp   16:9, subject right, left half dark
  assets/heroes/<page>/ipad.webp      4:3, same composition
  assets/heroes/<page>/iphone.webp    9:16, subject top, words beneath

Drop replacement PNGs into a folder of the same shape and run:

  python3 tools/heroes.py <folder>

It writes the WebP the page actually serves — the PNGs are around 2 MB
each and the WebP about 80 KB, which is the difference between a page
that opens and one that waits.

THE MARK
--------
assets/mark-flacon.svg is her own mark, drawn from the plaque on the
bottle she is holding in the photograph on lamaisondestefanie.com: the
flacon with its wide flat stopper, its ribbed collar and the plinth it
stands on. It replaces the invented "S" monogram that was on the header,
the footer and the opening curtain. The header and footer carry the same
shape inline so it can take the gold from the stylesheet; the curtain
uses the file.

The wordmark beside it is set the way her label sets it — title case in
the display serif with the "de" in italic — not as letterspaced capitals.

If a vector of the real mark turns up, replace assets/mark-flacon.svg and
the two inline copies in tools/shell.html, then run tools/pages.py.

CURRENT ARTWORK
---------------
The twelve shelf images and three location images in assets/img are
generated concept artwork. They are cohesive review assets, not verified
photographs of Stefanie's actual products, shop, or neighbourhood. Their
full-resolution generated source files are kept under
assets/source/generated-products/ and assets/source/generated-places/.
Replace a concept with a real photograph when available; preserve the
same crop, charcoal surface, and single warm side light for the twelve
shelf objects.

The product image slots retain their existing paths and are already
wired into content.json:

THE FIVE THREAD CARDS
---------------------
The five cards under "Choose what you remember" have no photograph and
no concept image. A thread is a question, not an object, so each card
carries a mark drawn in the same single gold line as the opening
curtain: a courtyard arch, the sea, someone standing there, a church
window, and — for the perfume that is gone — a bottle whose outline
never closes. They are vector, not artwork to be replaced.

If Stefanie wants a photograph behind a thread instead, fill that
thread's "image" field in content.json (Studio -> The bench) and the
drawn mark steps aside for it on its own.

THE FIRST SCREEN AS A FILM (hero-preview.html)
----------------------------------------------
Not on the home page. hero-preview.html is a noindex copy of the home
page whose only difference is the first screen, for approval before it
replaces the one that is live.

  assets/hero/plate-{1..4}-wide.webp   16:9, one locked plate per act
  assets/hero/plate-{1..4}-tall.webp   9:16, the phone's own composition
  assets/hero/petal-{1..6}.webp        petals cut out of the keyframes
  assets/hero-film.css                 the look of it
  assets/hero-film.js                  the player

The plates are locked frames — camera and subject do not move between
them, so nothing morphs. Everything that moves is built as its own layer
over the plate: petals that let go and turn as they fall, the ring a
petal leaves on the water, vapour off the still, a drop at a lip. Four
acts, cross-faded, 3.6s each with a 1.4s dissolve.

THE CEILING ON SHARPNESS. Every frame in the motion pack is 1280 x 720 —
all 64 of them, one stray 1600 x 900 aside. That is the whole of the
detail there is; nothing can put back what was never in the file. The
plates now resize once, take a real unsharp mask and are written at WebP
quality 90 rather than 78, which recovers about 40 per cent more edge
energy than the first pass and nearly doubles the bytes spent on the
still. The copper reads as copper again, but it is still a 720p source.

If the full-resolution originals exist, drop them in and run
tools/filmplates.py again — nothing else has to change, and the result
would be properly sharp rather than as sharp as 720p allows.

Rebuild the plates with tools/filmplates.py, the petals with
tools/petals.py, and the page itself with tools/preview.py.

HERO STORY AND MOTION
---------------------
The existing hero images and all nine frames of the bottle/petal loop
are preserved. The timing is now slower and overlapping so the petals
read as continuous motion:
  chapter dissolve: 1250 ms
  bottle/petal frame hold: 220 ms
  bottle/petal crossfade: 520 ms

The exact creative prompt and full hero asset map are in
docs/hero-motion-prompt.md. The original four full-resolution source
photographs were not in the repository or commit history. Their WebP
derivatives remain in assets/img, but cannot restore their original
resolution or grading latitude. Drop those originals into tools/supplied/
when available; that directory is now tracked.

FOUND, NOT YET USED
-------------------
lamaisondestefanie.com — the site still on her domain — carries one file
this repository does not, and it is the one the list above was missing:

  assets/house.mp4   2.4 MB, 9:16, real footage of Stefanie at her own
                     bench, in the shop, with the real products on the
                     counter and the amber stock bottles on the shelves
                     behind her.

It is daylight and documentary, so it cannot be dropped into a dark
cinematic page as it stands — it wants grading, and probably a slower
cut. Stills from it are also the only real photographs of the twelve
products that exist anywhere.

Its assets/stefanie_hero_loop.mp4 is the same generated bottle artwork
already noted below, not footage of her. Its stefanie-portrait.jpg is
the photograph already in this repository as assets/img/stefanie-751.webp.
  - Real shelf photographs of the twelve actual products, shot together
    on the same dark surface under one warm side light.
  - Real location photographs: doorway room/bench, Mnisikleous 10 at
    dusk, and the Plaka steps.
  - Optional 6–10 second clip of Stefanie working at the bench.

The referenced favicon, apple-touch icon, Open Graph image, hero chapter
WebPs, all nine bottle/petal frame WebPs, and both temporary hero-loop
video formats are present in the repository. The supplied video files
are temporary artwork and are not footage of Stefanie.
